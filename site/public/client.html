Client centre

Two steps, both from your phone.

No portal to log into and no password to remember. Each link below opens on its own.

Step one

Sign your agreement

Your service agreement and the scope of work for your plan, in one document. Read it, type your name, done — it works the same on a phone as on a laptop. You get a signed copy by email with a certificate showing when and from where it was signed.

Nothing starts, and nothing is charged, until this is signed.

Open my agreement

Your agreement link is sent to you by email. If you need it again, ask and it is re-sent within the hour.

Step two

Set up your monthly payment

One link, one time. Your plan runs month to month and renews on the same date each month until you stop it. You can change the card or bank account, see every past receipt, or cancel outright from that same link — without going through us.

Bank debit is the default because it costs less and we pass that on. Card is there if you prefer it.

Set up my payment

Your payment link is sent with your agreement. It is tied to the plan and price you agreed, so it cannot be set to the wrong amount.

How money works here — the short version

  • We never touch your customers' money. We do not run cards, take payments, or hold funds for you. When one of your customers owes you, you get a notice that an invoice is ready to be charged, and you charge it. That is written into your agreement, not just promised here.
  • We are not responsible for any money transaction of yours. No chargebacks, no disputes, no refunds pass through us, because nothing of yours passes through us.
  • The one exception is our own fee. Your monthly payment for this service runs on our own payment processor, which is what the link above sets up. That is the only money we ever handle, and it is ours, not yours.
  • We bookkeep. We do not do your accounting. We are not CPAs and we do not file, advise on, or sign anything tax related.
  • You approve anything that matters. Nothing with money, commitment, or your name on it goes out without you saying yes first.

Something wrong?

Reply to any email we have sent you and it reaches us directly. If a link above does not open, say so — a broken link is our problem to fix, not yours to work around.
