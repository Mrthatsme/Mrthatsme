How money works here — the short version

  • We never touch your customers' money. We do not run cards, take payments, or hold funds for you. When one of your customers owes you, you get a notice that an invoice is ready to be charged, and you charge it. That is written into your agreement, not just promised here.
  • We are not responsible for any money transaction of yours. No chargebacks, no disputes, no refunds pass through us, because nothing of yours passes through us.
  • The one exception is our own fee. Your monthly payment for this service runs on our own payment processor, which is what the link above sets up. That is the only money we ever handle, and it is ours, not yours.
  • We bookkeep. We do not do your accounting. We are not CPAs and we do not file, advise on, or sign anything tax related.
  • You approve anything that matters. Nothing with money, commitment, or your name on it goes out without you saying yes first.
