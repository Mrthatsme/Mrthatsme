Where it starts

Bring us the thing that keeps falling through.

You don't need to know whether it's a people problem or a software problem. Tell us what's slipping. The quotes nobody circles back on. The calls that come in while everyone's on a job. The invoices sitting out there unpaid. The inbox nobody has been to the bottom of since spring.

We'll tell you what it would take to close that gap, and then we close it. Sometimes that's a person doing the work. Usually it ends up being an automation doing it every day, built in your own accounts, with wording you approved.

"Can you clean up our inbox and make sure customer requests actually get followed up?"

Week oneWe sort the inbox, flag what needs a decision, and start a running list of every open customer request so nothing sits.

By month twoThe sorting and the follow-up reminders run on their own, using rules you approved.

"We send quotes and then forget about them."

Week oneWe pull every open quote into one list and follow them up by hand, in your words.

By month twoTRACE does it on day 2, 5 and 10, and stops the second they reply.

"People book and then don't show."

Week oneWe confirm every appointment the day before and tell you which ones look shaky.

By month twoConfirmations and reminders go out automatically, and anyone who no-shows gets a follow-up the same day.

That's the model. A person does it first, so you can watch it done your way before anything runs on its own. Then the parts that repeat become automations, and the hours go back to the things that actually need a human.
