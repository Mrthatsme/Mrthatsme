Questions

What owners like you ask.

More answers on the main questions page.

Do you give legal advice?

No. We handle administrative work only. If a client or prospect asks a legal question, the reply says we can't answer it and the question goes straight to you.

Do you handle trust accounting?

No. We never touch a trust or IOLTA account or any client funds. Billing reminders only link to the payment page you already use, and you or your bookkeeper handle the money.

Who is responsible for deadlines?

You are, always. We can keep your calendar current, but a court date or deadline only goes on the calendar after you confirm it, and we never calculate one ourselves.

Will you work in our practice-management software?

We work in the tools you already use by being added as a user, the same way you'd add a new staff member. We confirm your specific software on the free assessment call before you sign anything.

Is our client information kept confidential?

Yes. Your information stays in your own accounts, is never mixed with another client's, and is never used to train any AI model. Every action is logged so you can see who did what and when.

Do you text our clients?

No, never. Replies happen where the person reached out, like your website chat or email.
