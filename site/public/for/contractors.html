Questions

What owners like you ask.

More answers on the services page and the pricing page.

Do you send my crews out?

Not on our own. Each evening we line up tomorrow's schedule and flag conflicts. Once you approve it, each crew member gets their schedule by email. If your crews already get texts from your own scheduling app, like Jobber or Housecall Pro, we can send the schedule through that app instead. Nothing ever comes from a number of ours.

Do you text my customers?

Not from a number of ours, ever. Replies happen where the customer reached out, like your website chat or email. If your own software already has customer messaging built in, the reply can go through that, from your account, in words you approved, and only to someone who contacted you first. We confirm which of your tools can do that on the assessment call. Anyone who replies STOP, or asks not to be contacted in any other way, never hears from it again. Missed calls go to the top of your brief so you can call back.

What about emergencies after hours?

You tell us what counts as an emergency and who's on call. An emergency goes straight to that person, and the customer gets a short holding reply so they're not left waiting.

Do you order supplies?

No. We flag when you're due to reorder, based on your purchase history. You decide what to buy and place the order.

Will it work with Jobber or Housecall Pro?

We work in the tools you already use by being added as a user. We confirm your specific software on the free assessment call before you sign anything.
