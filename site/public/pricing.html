Questions

What owners usually ask us.

What do you actually do?

We find the repetitive work eating your week, like missed calls, quote follow-ups, reminders and invoices, then build automations to handle it. Our agents run them daily and fix anything that breaks. Building your first automations is included. No setup fee.

What happens if an automation breaks?

We monitor every automation we build, and we fix breaks at no charge, usually within one business day and often before you notice. Repairs never use your hours.

If I cancel, do I lose the automations?

No. They live in your own accounts and keep running. We just stop monitoring and maintaining them.

How many hours do I get?

Every plan includes a set number of support hours: 10, 22 or 40, used for new builds and hands-on admin. We count each task at the time a skilled assistant would take (for example, 5 minutes to schedule an appointment), and you get a monthly report showing every task. We never bill extra hours without asking first.

Is software included?

Yes. The tools we use to serve you are included in your plan, up to an allowance. Your own existing subscriptions, like your email or accounting software, stay yours.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans: cancel any time with 30 days' notice. Retainers are paid upfront for 12 months at 10% off. Leave early and we refund the rest of the year, keeping only the 10% discount on the months you used. The first month covers setup and isn't refundable once setup begins.

Who does the work?

AI agents do the work, set up and supervised by a person, and nothing important goes out without your approval level.

Will my customers feel like they're talking to a robot?

Messages are written in your words, and you approve the wording before anything goes out. If a customer asks who they're talking to, it tells them it's your company's assistant. It never claims to be you.

Can it take payments or handle money?

No. It has no access to your bank accounts and never takes, sends or refunds money for you. It never runs a card — if one needs charging, you get told and you charge it. We only bill our own monthly plan.

What happens if it gets something wrong?

Every action gets checked after it runs, and anything that fails shows up in your daily brief. You can pause everything with one switch, and nothing is lost while it's paused.

Do I need to buy new software?

No. We connect to what you already use — Gmail, Google Calendar, your website forms and most CRMs — and any tools we add to serve you are included in your plan, up to its software allowance.

What if my internet goes down, or there's a storm?

It keeps working. We work on your email and calendar online, where they already live. Your mail keeps arriving and keeps getting handled, and it's all waiting for you when you're back.

Is my customer data safe?

We connect by being added as a user to your tools, not by taking your passwords, give the system only the access it needs, and log every action. Your data stays in your own accounts, and your information is never mixed with another client's.

What if I want to stop?

Your data and your automations stay in your own accounts. You can also revoke our access yourself, instantly, without asking us. See “Can I cancel?” above for notice and retainer terms.
