Questions.

Is there a setup fee?

No, not on the monthly plans. Custom Builds are priced per job after we map the process.

Do you bill by the hour?

No. Essential and Business Support are flat monthly prices. Hands-on assistant work has a fair-use limit, set out in the service agreement.

What if I need more help one month?

We tell you before doing anything extra. You can move the work to next month, have us automate the part that repeats, move up a plan, or approve a fixed price for the extra work in writing.

What's a founding client?

One of our first 3 Business Support clients. Your price stays locked, and your first process map is free.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. Everything we built stays in your accounts.

What if something breaks?

You're told in plain words, and it's fixed within 1 business day at no charge.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up or down at the start of any month.
