Five steps to go live.

  1. 1

    Free 30-minute call

    We talk through where your time goes. You leave with a written plan, whether you sign up or not.

  2. 2

    We sign the NDA and map it

    Confidentiality is signed before we see a single email. Then we map each job step by step, in your words.

  3. 3

    We build inside your accounts

    Your email, calendar and website. You add us as a user, the way you'd add an employee. Nothing is locked on our side, and you get a written guide for every setup.

  4. 4

    Two weeks read-only

    Nothing goes to your customers. Each morning you read what it would have said, and we adjust. Most of the tuning happens here.

  5. 5

    Go live, your way

    You pick a level for every task. Prices, dates and promises always wait for your OK.

Questions.

What happens on the free call?

We walk through where your week goes, pick the one or two jobs that cost you the most, and you get a written plan by email. It's yours to keep.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

No. You add us as a user in each tool, the way you'd add an employee, and you can remove us from the same place any time. Banking, card and payroll logins are never shared.

What happens during the two weeks read-only?

Nothing goes out. Every morning you see what would have been sent, word for word, and we adjust it until it sounds like you.

What about emergencies?

If you have someone on call, emergencies go straight to them, any hour. You decide what counts as an emergency and who's on call when. The customer gets a short note that your team has been alerted.

Can I pause everything?

Yes. One switch in your morning report pauses everything, and you can turn it back on whenever you like.

Get your minutes back.

A free 30-minute call with Jerome. You keep the written plan either way.
