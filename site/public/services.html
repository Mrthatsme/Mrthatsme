You're in control

You write the rules. It follows them.

Before anything goes live, you decide how much the AI can do on its own. Change any setting whenever you want, or pause everything with one switch.

Every job gets checked

  • Verified, not assumed. After booking an appointment, we confirm the calendar has the right customer, place and time, and that the confirmation was delivered.
  • Mistakes show up. Anything that fails is flagged in your brief. Nothing gets quietly marked done.
  • Full record. Every action and every approval is logged with who, what and when.
  • Secure approvals. You approve from a signed-in page. Nobody can approve by texting back "yes."

What it will never do

  • Touch your money. No payments, refunds or deposits, and no card ever run. No access to your bank. We only bill our own monthly plan.
  • Set prices. No quotes, discounts or payment terms without your approval.
  • Give legal, tax or insurance advice. Those questions go straight to you.
  • Make promises. No warranties, dates or guarantees you didn't approve in writing.
  • Pretend to be you. If a customer asks, it says it's your company's assistant.
