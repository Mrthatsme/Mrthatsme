The five automations

Five things, named. Here's what the help turns into.

These are the jobs we take off you first, because they're the ones every business repeats. Each is built in your own accounts, runs every day, and gets fixed by us if it breaks. You approve the wording before any of it goes live.

01

Lead Catcher

Someone messages your site at 7pm. They get a real answer in minutes, in words you approved, and the lead lands on tomorrow's brief with their name, number and what they want. It never quotes a price and never promises a date. It doesn't answer your phone: missed calls go to the top of your brief so you can call back.

02

Quote Chaser

You send an estimate and forget it. It doesn't. Follow-ups go out on day 2, day 5 and day 10, and stop the moment the customer replies. You only hear about the replies.

03

Schedule Keeper

Confirmations the day before, reminders the morning of, and a follow-up on anyone who no-shows. Reschedules get handled without a phone call. Emergencies go straight to whoever is on call.

04

Review Builder

The day a job closes out, the customer gets asked for a review. One ask, politely worded, never a second one. The brief shows you who left one.

05

Invoice & Chase

The invoice goes out when the job closes, and the unpaid ones get chased on a schedule so nobody is doing it at 10pm. You see who's paid and who hasn't. We send and we follow up. We never take the money. When something needs collecting, you collect it.

How the hours are counted. Your hours are a person's time. Each task is counted at the time a skilled assistant would take, even when AI tools help get it done faster, so booking an appointment is five minutes, not an hour. Your monthly report lists every one. Approved automations never use your hours however often they run, and neither do monitoring, repairs or your daily brief. Unused hours roll over a month.

And the two sides trade places over time. The longer we work together, the more of the left column turns into an automation, and the more of your hours go to the right one. That's the point. You end up with a person for the judgment calls and a system for everything else.

Questions

What owners usually ask us.

Nothing you type here leaves your browser.

What happens if an automation breaks?

We monitor every automation we build. When one breaks, it shows up in your daily brief with what failed, and we fix it at no charge, usually within one business day. Repairs never use your hours.

Is my customer data safe?

We connect by being added as a user to your tools, not by taking your passwords. We give the system only the access it needs and log every action. Your data stays in your own accounts, and your information is never mixed with another client's. You can revoke our access yourself, instantly, without asking us.

Do you need my password?

No. You don't hand us your passwords, and there's nothing to install on your computer or phone. You add us the way you'd add a new employee: Gmail calls it “grant access,” and Google Calendar, your Google listing, Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks and Square each have a team or user page. You choose what we can see and remove us yourself in seconds. The one exception, and only if you choose it: if one of your tools has no way to add a user, you can share that one login through 1Password, from your own vault or into a vault we keep for you alone that you can see and empty at any time. A person uses it, never the automated system. We never accept a bank, card or payroll login, or the login to your main email account, and you change any shared password when we part ways. The steps are at minutesregained.com/access.

Will my customers feel like they're talking to a robot?

Messages are written in your words, and you approve the wording before anything goes out. If a customer asks who they're talking to, it tells them it's your company's assistant. It never claims to be you.

Do you answer our phones?

No. We don't answer calls and we don't run an AI receptionist. Lead Catcher replies to your website chat, forms and email, in words you approved, and missed calls go to the top of your daily brief so you can call back. If you need every call answered live, that's an answering service, not us.

Do you handle our money?

No. We send your invoices and chase the unpaid ones, and that is where it stops. No access to your bank, no card ever run, no payment taken, sent or refunded. When something needs collecting you get told, and you collect it. The only money we ever handle is our own monthly fee.

Which AI do you use, and what can it see?

Drafts are written by Claude, from Anthropic. For each draft it sees the one message being answered and your approved wording, and Anthropic does not train on it. Voice notes are turned into text by Cloudflare. To connect Gmail we ask Google for permission to read and organize your mail and save drafts; Google's name for that permission also covers sending, but our system has no send function and never sends from your mailbox. On Microsoft we ask only to read and write mail, not to send. The full list is on our privacy page.
