Hard limits

What it never does.

  • Run a card, take a payment or issue a refund
  • Log into your bank
  • Threaten collections or add late fees you didn't set
  • Chase anyone you've marked 'hold'

Whatever you set, it never touches your money, never texts from a number of ours, and never messages anyone who didn't contact you first or who asked to stop.
