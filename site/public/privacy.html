Privacy

Effective October 22, 2026

Minutes Regained is operated by Provident Watch LLC. This page explains what we collect, what we do with it, and what we never do. Questions: support@minutesregained.com.

What we collect

  • When you contact us: the name, business, email, phone and message you type into a form on this site.
  • When you sign up: your name, email and business, the plan you chose, and a record of your signature (the time, your internet address, your browser, and a fingerprint of the exact agreement text).
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. You enter your card or bank details on Stripe's page; we never see or store them.
  • If you connect your email (clients only): access to new messages in the one mailbox you connect, permission to save reply drafts and add labels, and that mailbox's address.

How we use your email, if you connect it

  • We read new messages that arrive after you connect, to draft replies and label what needs you.
  • We save draft replies in your own Drafts folder. You decide whether to send them.
  • To write a draft, the text of that one message is processed by our AI provider. It is used only to produce your draft and is not used to train any AI model.
  • We keep a short activity log (sender, subject, and what we did) so you can see every action. We do not keep copies of your messages.

What we never do

  • We never send, forward or delete your email. On Microsoft accounts we don't even ask for permission to send.
  • We never sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the providers needed to run the service (Cloudflare for hosting, Stripe for payments, Resend for our notification emails, and our AI provider for drafting).
  • We never use one client's information for another client's work.
  • No person at Minutes Regained reads your email, except to fix a problem you ask us to look at, for security, or where the law requires it.

Minutes Regained's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Access to your mailbox is stored encrypted (AES-256) and kept separate for each client. Everything travels over encrypted connections.

Disconnecting and deleting

You can disconnect at any time, without asking us: remove "Minutes Regained" from your Google Account (Security > Third-party connections) or your Microsoft account (Privacy > Apps and services). Access stops immediately. Email support@minutesregained.com and we will delete your stored access and activity log within five business days, beyond what tax and business records require.

Cookies

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. We use privacy-friendly visit counts that do not identify you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. Connected clients are emailed about any change to how we handle their email.
