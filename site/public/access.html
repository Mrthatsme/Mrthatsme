Client centre · Connect your tools

Add us the way you'd add a new employee.

Your scheduling, invoicing and listing tools each have a place to add a team member. You add us there, you choose what we can see, and you remove us from the same screen whenever you like. Most take two minutes.

Before you start

  • Use the address in your setup email. Every invite goes to an address we set up for your business only, so your access is never mixed with another client's.
  • Pick the access level we name. Your setup email lists, for each tool, the narrowest level that covers the work you chose. If a tool asks about money, payroll or pay rates, leave it off.
  • Only the tools you use. Skip anything below that isn't part of your business.
  • Taking it back. Remove us from the same team or user list. When you cancel, we confirm in writing within five business days that no access remains.

Step by step, tool by tool

Menu names move around when these companies update their apps. If a step doesn't match what you see, reply to your setup email with a screenshot and we'll walk you through it.

Email (Gmail or Outlook)
  1. Open the connect link in your setup email.
  2. Choose Google or Microsoft and sign in on their own page. We never see your password.
  3. Approve the access it lists. That's it.

To remove it: in your Google Account, Security, then Third-party connections; in Microsoft, My Apps. Drafts are written for you to send. Nothing goes out from your inbox on its own.

Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com.
  2. Next to your work calendar, click the three dots, then Settings and sharing.
  3. Under Share with specific people, click Add people, enter our address, choose the permission from your setup email, and click Send.
Google Business Profile (your Google listing)
  1. Signed in as the owner, search for your business name on Google and open your profile's menu.
  2. Open Business Profile settings, then People and access (some accounts call it Managers).
  3. Click Add, enter our address, choose Manager, and send the invite.

A Manager can answer reviews and update hours and posts. A Manager can't remove you, add anyone else or delete the listing.

Jobber
  1. Click the gear icon, then Manage Team.
  2. Click Add User and enter a name and our address.
  3. Choose the permission level from your setup email, then click Save to send the invite.

Jobber's Core plan includes one extra user. If yours is already taken, tell us before you change plans.

Housecall Pro
  1. On a computer (the phone app can't set permissions), open Account Settings, then Employees.
  2. Click +, enter our address, and choose Office Staff.
  3. Tick only the permissions in your setup email. Leave View and edit employee pay details off, then save.
QuickBooks Online
  1. Click the gear icon, then Manage users, then Add user.
  2. Choose the user type from your setup email (usually a limited one, such as customers and vendors only, or reports only).
  3. Enter our address and send the invitation.

Reports-only users don't count toward your plan's user limit; other user types do. We never need banking or payroll access.

Square
  1. In your Square Dashboard, go to Staff, then Team members, then Add team member.
  2. Enter a name and our address.
  3. Choose the permission set from your setup email (never full access), then send the invite.
ServiceTitan
  1. Go to Settings, then People, then Employees, and click Add.
  2. Enter our address and choose the role from your setup email.
  3. Save.

An office user can add to your ServiceTitan bill. Check with your ServiceTitan rep first.

Your website (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace)
  1. WordPress: in the dashboard, go to Users, then Add New User; enter our address and choose Editor.
  2. Wix: in Settings, go to Roles & Permissions, then Invite People.
  3. Squarespace: in Settings, go to Permissions & Ownership, then Invite contributor.
A tool that isn't listed

Tell us its name. Almost every business tool has a team or user page, and we'll send you its steps.

If a tool has no way to add a user

A few tools, such as some supplier or permit portals, have one login and no team page. For those only, you share that one login through 1Password instead of by text or email.

  • If you already use 1Password: invite us as a guest to a vault holding just that login, or send it as a one-time item link that only our address can open.
  • If you don't: we send you an invite to a vault we keep for your business alone. You join it free as a guest, add the login, and can see everything in it and remove anything at any time.
  • A person uses it, never the automated system. In a vault we keep, every time a login is opened is logged.
  • If the tool texts you a code, we agree at setup whether the code comes to you or is kept with the login.
  • When we part ways, we delete the vault, send you the list of logins it held so you can change those passwords, and confirm in writing.

What we never take

Bank, card or payroll logins, and the login to your main email account. None of them is needed for anything we do, and all of them are written out of your agreement. Never send a password by text, email or a form.
