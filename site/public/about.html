About Minutes Regained

You'll be working with Jerome.

Minutes Regained is one person, not a call center and not an offshore team. I set up every client myself, and I'm the one you email when something's wrong.

I spent 20 years and 6 months in the Navy as an Operations Specialist, which is the job of watching everything happening at once and making sure the right person hears about the right thing at the right time. That's the same job your back office needs, just with quotes and invoices instead of aircraft. I'm also a Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt, which is a long way of saying I get paid to find the step in your week that shouldn't exist and delete it.

I work with small businesses anywhere in the country.

  • I set up your account. Your assessment, your rules and your two-week read-only trial are done with me, not handed to someone else.
  • I watch the work. Every automation checks in daily. When one fails, I get alerted and I fix it, usually within one business day, at no charge.
  • I do your hands-on admin. The hours in your plan are my work, done with AI tools and checked before anything reaches you or a customer.
  • I sign an NDA. Every engagement starts with one, and I'll sign yours instead if you'd rather.

"You're one guy. What happens if you're not around?"

Fair question, and the answer is built into how this works.

  • Everything runs in your accounts, not mine. If I vanished tomorrow, your automations keep running. They don't live on my server and they don't need me logged in.
  • Everything is documented. You get a written map of what was built and why, plus the recording of every build session. Another person could pick it up from those documents. That's the point of them.
  • You can cut my access in thirty seconds, from your own admin console, without asking me.
  • What would actually stop is new work. Not the running of what's already there. That's the honest version, and it's why the documentation isn't an afterthought.
