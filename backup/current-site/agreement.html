MINUTES REGAINED — SERVICE AGREEMENT

Provider: Minutes Regained, operated by Provident Watch LLC
          7901 4th St N #36617, St. Petersburg, FL 33702
          support@minutesregained.com · (877) 896-8784
Client: [Your business]
Plan: Business Support
Price: $1,300/month for the Business Support plan.
Billing: Monthly, in advance, on the same day of the month as the start date, by
card on file via Stripe. Month to month.
No setup fee.
Included each month:
  - All 5 core automations, plus 1 custom automation, built, run and monitored. The core automations are FIRST (new leads), TRACE (quote follow-up), CLOCK (scheduling), STARS (reviews) and PAID (invoices)
  - 8 hours per month of hands-on admin and new builds (unused hours roll over 1 month)
  - Additional hours only with Client's prior written approval, at $95/hour
  - Software used to serve Client included, up to $100/month
  - 2 inboxes managed
  - 250 voice notes per month written up; beyond that, $10 for each additional 100 (or part of 100), added to the next monthly invoice (for a Retainer, invoiced separately for that month). Unused voice notes don't roll over
  - 30-minute monthly review call
  - Daily brief. Monitoring, repairs and the daily brief never use Client's hours

1. Term and cancellation. This agreement runs month to month. Either party
   may cancel with thirty (30) days' written notice. There is no long-term
   commitment.

2. Scope of control. Client sets and may change, at any time, what Minutes
   Regained's systems are authorized to do, using the four control levels
   published on minutesregained.com (Handle it / Tell me / Ask me / Never).
   No action with a real-world consequence to Client's business or its
   customers is taken without the authorization level Client has set.
   Client may also pause everything at any time, from the link in the daily
   brief: while paused, Client's inbox is not read and nothing is drafted,
   routed or sent on Client's behalf, and requests through Client's form go
   to Client unprocessed. Pausing does not change the fee or the notice
   period under section 1, and Client may resume at any time.

3. No financial access on Client's behalf. The payment set up through this
   agreement is Client's fee to Minutes Regained for the service
   described above — nothing more. Minutes Regained will never request or
   store any banking credential or payment login belonging to Client, will
   never have the ability to transfer Client's funds, and will never charge,
   collect, hold, or move money on behalf of Client or Client's customers.
   Where Client's plan includes PAID, Minutes Regained sends
   invoices from Client's own invoicing tool, for amounts Client has set,
   follows up on unpaid ones and reads payment status there. It never
   takes, moves or refunds a payment and never runs a card. Every payment
   is collected by Client directly, through Client's own systems.

4. Access and revocation. Client grants access through the user, team-
   member or delegated-access features of the platforms Client already uses
   (e.g. adding Minutes Regained as a team member, or Google "grant
   access"). Where a platform has no such feature, Client may instead share
   that platform's login through a password manager, either from Client's
   own vault or into a vault Minutes Regained keeps for Client alone, which
   Client can view and remove access from at any time. A shared login is
   used only by a person at Minutes Regained, never by its automated
   systems, and is never a banking, card or payroll login or the login to
   Client's primary email account. Client may revoke any access at
   any time, directly, without needing Minutes Regained's involvement. Upon
   cancellation, Minutes Regained will delete every shared login it holds
   for Client, will list them so Client can change those passwords, and
   will confirm in writing within five (5)
   business days that no access and no copy of Client's data remains,
   beyond what tax and business recordkeeping requires. Automations built
   for Client live in Client's own accounts and keep running after
   cancellation; Minutes Regained simply stops monitoring and maintaining
   them.

5. Data handling. Client's data is kept separate from every other client's
   data. It is used only to perform Client's work. It is not pooled across
   clients and is not used to train any model.

6. Fees and refunds. $1,300/month for the Business Support plan. This fee is the only payment
   Minutes Regained collects under this agreement, apart from additional
   hours Client has approved in writing in advance at the rate above.
   Minutes Regained never charges or collects, on Client's behalf, any
   amount owed to Client by Client's own customers. Software Minutes Regained uses to serve
   Client is included up to the monthly allowance above; Client's own
   existing subscriptions (such as email or accounting software) remain
   Client's, and no software cost above the allowance is billed to Client.
   Support hours cover a person's hands-on work: new builds and one-off
   admin. Automations Client has approved to run on their own never use
   Client's hours, and neither do monitoring, repairs or the daily brief.
   Each hands-on task is counted at the time a skilled assistant would
   take, and Client receives a monthly report listing every task. There is
   no setup fee. The first month covers setup and is not refundable once
   setup begins.

7. No guarantee of uptime. Minutes Regained commits to a one-business-day
   response time, not an uptime percentage. Service interruptions delay
   work; they do not cause an unauthorized action, because nothing with a
   real consequence is sent without Client's prior authorization under the
   control level Client has set.

8. Entire agreement. This document, together with the published control
   levels and FAQ at minutesregained.com referenced above, is the entire
   agreement between the parties for this engagement.
