Questions.

What does a virtual assistant do for my business?

The work that needs a person: cleaning up your inbox and replying to email, managing your calendar, scheduling appointments, customer follow-ups, data entry, documents and one-off projects. It's done inside your own accounts.

Do I have to use automation?

No. Essential and Business Support are virtual assistant plans, so you can start with just the assistant. When we notice a task that happens the same way every time, we'll suggest automating it, and you decide.

Do you answer my phone?

No. Missed calls show up in your morning report, ranked, so you know who to call back first. Website and social messages do get answered.

What about emergencies?

If you have someone on call, emergencies go straight to them, any hour. You decide what counts as an emergency and who's on call when. The customer gets a short note that your team has been alerted, and it shows on your morning report.

Will anything go out that I didn't approve?

Only wording you've signed off on. Anything with a price, a date or a promise waits for you.

What if you're out sick or on vacation?

Everything keeps running. It lives in your accounts, not on our computers, every setup is written down, and we get an alert if something breaks.

What tools do I need?

Usually the ones you already have: your email, calendar and website. We build inside your accounts.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. Everything we built stays yours.
