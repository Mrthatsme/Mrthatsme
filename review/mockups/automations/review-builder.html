How it starts

A person first. Then it runs.

Week one
We send each request by hand the day the job closes, so you can see the timing and wording.
Weeks one and two · shadow watch
The automation drafts everything and sends nothing. You read every word.
By month two
Requests go out on their own when a job closes. Who left a review shows up in your turnover.

Hard limits

What it never does.

  • Ask twice
  • Offer anything in exchange for a review
  • Ask only the happy customers (Google doesn't allow it, and it's not honest)
  • Post or reply to reviews without your OK

Whatever you set, it never touches your money, never texts from a number of ours, and never messages anyone who didn't contact you first or who asked to stop.
