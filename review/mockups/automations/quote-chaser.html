How it starts

A person first. Then it runs.

Week one
We pull every open quote into one list and follow them up by hand, in your words.
Weeks one and two · read-only trial
Everything is drafted and nothing is sent, so you see exactly what it would have done.
By month two
It runs on day 2, 5 and 10 on its own, and stops the moment anyone replies.

Hard limits

What it never does.

  • Change a price or offer a discount
  • Keep chasing after someone says no or asks to stop
  • Send a fourth message. Three and done.
  • Chase a quote you marked closed

Whatever you set, it never touches your money, never texts from a number of ours, and never messages anyone who didn't contact you first or who asked to stop.
