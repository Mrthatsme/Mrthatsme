How it starts

A person first. Then it runs.

Week one
We confirm every appointment the day before and tell you which ones look shaky.
Weeks one and two · read-only trial
Everything is drafted and nothing is sent, so you see exactly what it would have done.
By month two
Confirmations, reminders and no-show follow-ups go out on their own.

Hard limits

What it never does.

  • Send a crew anywhere without your OK on the schedule
  • Book outside the hours and rules you set
  • Text from a number of ours
  • Override an appointment you placed by hand

Whatever you set, it never touches your money, never texts from a number of ours, and never messages anyone who didn't contact you first or who asked to stop.

Questions

Does it text my customers?

Only through your own scheduling app, if it already has texting built in (Jobber and Housecall Pro can). Never from a number of ours.

What about my crews?

Each evening we line up tomorrow and flag conflicts. Once you approve, each crew gets its schedule.

CLOCK is built in your own accounts, checked daily and repaired free.
