How your hours are counted

Your hours cover a person's hands-on work: one-off admin and new builds, including the custom automation that comes with Business Support. Build time counts from the first conversation about what you need to the day it's finished, and if a build is heading past your hours, we tell you and ask before going over. Each task is counted at the time a skilled assistant would take, even when AI tools help get it done faster, and your monthly report lists every one. Unused hours roll over one month.

Questions

Questions about plans and hours.

How many hours do I get?

Essential includes 4 hours a month and Business Support 8 hours of a person's time: building new automations, and one-off admin you hand us, like rescheduling a week of jobs or cleaning up your contact list. Automations you've approved never use your hours, however often they run, and neither do monitoring, repairs or your daily brief. Each task a person does for you is counted at the time a skilled assistant would take, even when AI tools help get it done faster (for example, 5 minutes to schedule an appointment), and your monthly report lists every one. Unused hours roll over one month. Extra hours are $95 an hour on either plan, and we never bill them without asking first.

Can I cancel?

Essential and Business Support: cancel any time with 30 days' notice. A 12-month Retainer is paid upfront at 10% off; leave early and we refund the rest of the year, keeping only the 10% discount on the months you used. Custom Automation has a 12-month term, because the build happens in the first month, and is monthly after that. On every plan, the first month covers the build and isn't refundable once building begins.

If I cancel, do I lose the automations?

No. They live in your own accounts and keep running. We just stop monitoring and maintaining them.

Do I need to buy new software?

No. We connect to what you already use, like Gmail, Google Calendar, your website forms and most CRMs, and any tools we add to serve you are included up to your plan's software allowance.
