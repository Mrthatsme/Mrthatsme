# Reference: AI agent layout for a real estate agent (saved Nov 12, 2026)

**Status: stored for later. Do not copy.** Jerome saved this as an example of how a realtor's back office could be laid
out, for when Minutes Regained gets a real estate client. It is someone else's design. Use it for ideas only, and build
anything for a realtor in our own words, names and look. The original image is kept out of this public repo.

## What the layout shows (summarized in our words)

- **Top:** the realtor, with one coordinator under them ("Chief of Staff") that routes email and tasks, keeps one summary
  of what needs the realtor's OK, and keeps a task board current.
- **Six specialist agents** under the coordinator:
  1. Transaction and operations: realtor forms, deal dates, milestone updates, signing after OK, compliance forms, collapsed deals
  2. Email and documents: files signed documents, sends links, flags unsigned forms, files receipts, checks for missing items
  3. Seller and listing: seller details, seller pipeline, showing feedback, cancel and re-list forms, hands listings to marketing
  4. Buyer and lead: buyer details, real leads into the CRM, welcome emails, leads not yet contacted, showing updates
  5. Marketing and design: listing photos, Just Listed and Sold posts, marketplace drafts, social posts, brochures on request
  6. Follow-up and relationships: past-client check-ins, home anniversaries, birthdays
- **Client intake** feeds new client details to the transaction agent; forms are filled and sent for signing after approval.
- **Shared tools:** email, drive, calendar, CRM, realtor forms and e-signature, showing service, Canva, Facebook and
  Instagram, MLS, task board.
- **18 automations in five groups:** leads and CRM; paperwork and signing; deal dates; filing and compliance; marketing
  and relationships.
- **Human in the loop:** nothing is sent, signed or posted without the realtor's approval.

## How it would map to Minutes Regained (notes for later)

- The coordinator is close to our **GM**; the daily summary is our **daily brief**, the weekly view is the **SITREP**.
- Leads map to **FIRST**, follow-ups to **TRACE**, showings and dates to **CLOCK**, past-client reviews to **STARS**.
  Commission and invoices are not like trades invoicing, so **PAID** may not fit as is.
- Already fits our rules: approval before anything goes out, permission levels, the Never list.

## Check before selling this to a realtor

- **Our never-list still applies:** no texts from a number of ours, no messages to anyone who didn't make contact first.
  Social posts and listing posts would be drafts the realtor publishes.
- **"FINTRAC" in the layout is Canadian** (anti-money-laundering reporting). A US realtor has different rules by state;
  confirm what applies before naming any compliance form.
- **Integrations are unverified:** MLS, realtor form systems (and their e-signature), showing services. Per CLAUDE.md, we
  only claim what we've confirmed; everything else is "we check it on the assessment call."
- **Fair housing:** listing descriptions and ads must avoid fair-housing problems; any listing-copy drafting needs that
  rule built in, and the realtor approves every word.
- **Licensed work stays with the realtor:** we handle admin, never advice on price, offers or contracts (same line we hold
  with law firms).
