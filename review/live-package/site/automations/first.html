Hard limits

What it never does.

  • Quote a price or give a range
  • Promise an arrival time or a date
  • Answer your phone (missed calls go to the top of your daily brief)
  • Pretend to be you. It says it's your company's assistant.

Whatever you set, it never touches your money, never texts from a number of ours, and never messages anyone who didn't contact you first or who asked to stop.

Questions

Does it answer my phone?

No. Missed calls go to the top of your daily brief so you can call back. If you need every call answered live, that's an answering service, not us.

What if someone has an emergency?

You tell us what counts as one and who's on call. It goes straight to that person, and the customer gets a short holding reply.
