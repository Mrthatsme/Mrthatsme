# 01: Wording rewrites (DRAFT, not live) · v2, plain English

**Goal:** stop sounding like the other AI-built automation site and sound like Minutes Regained: plain, calm, honest, and easy for **any business owner** to read.

**What changed in v2:** your readers are civilian business owners, not veterans. The military terms are gone from the page copy (shadow watch, on deck, watch turnover, SITREP, EXERCISE, 24-hour time). The Navy story stays where it belongs: on the About page, and in the tagline "Always on watch."

**How to use this:** each row shows the line on the live site now, the near-identical line on the other site, and a proposed replacement. Mark each row **Keep / Use mine / Rewrite**. Nothing changes on minutesregained.com until you approve.

**Voice rules:**
- Write for a busy plumber or office manager reading on a phone between jobs.
- Short sentences. Say what happens, then what never happens.
- No jargon of any kind: no military terms, no tech terms ("webhook," "agent," "connector").
- Military background shows up as *credibility* (About page, one line on Services), not as vocabulary.
