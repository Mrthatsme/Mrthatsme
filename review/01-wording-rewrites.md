# 01: Wording rewrites (DRAFT, not live)

**Goal:** stop sounding like every other AI-built automation site and sound like Minutes Regained. That means plain, calm, and written by someone who has stood a watch.

**How to use this:** each row shows the line on the live site now, the near-identical line on the other site (so you can see why it needs to change), and a proposed replacement. Mark each row **Keep / Use mine / Rewrite**. Nothing changes on minutesregained.com until you approve.

**Voice rules I wrote to:**
- Navy references stay light and earned: watch, turnover, SITREP, sign-off. No "roger that," no "mission-ready."
- Short sentences. Say what happens, then what never happens.
- "You" means the owner. "We" means Minutes Regained. Jerome's name shows up where a real person matters.
